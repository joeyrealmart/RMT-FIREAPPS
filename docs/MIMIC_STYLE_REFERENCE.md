# RMT Mimic Style Reference

## Located Visual Benchmark

The earlier clean LTK CAD-pilot images are available under `outputs/mimic-cad-pilot/ltk/`, including `LTK_MAIN_PANEL_OVERALL.png`, `LTK_FEED_MILL.png`, and `LTK_EGG_PRODUCTION.png`.

They are **style references only**. Their hard-coded/oversimplified geometry is not trusted and must never replace source-derived geometry.

## Approved Visual Characteristics

- White or very light background with generous whitespace.
- Simple dark structural linework with consistent weight.
- Low visual density and minimal wording.
- Clear source-confirmed walls, partitions, doors, stairs, useful beams, corridors, and navigation landmarks.
- Readable at phone/tablet scale without engineering-document clutter.
- No handwriting, arrows, annotation circles, dimensions, title blocks, obsolete pin marks, or scan noise.

The target is a technician navigation map, not a construction drawing. The original PDF remains geometry authority; the benchmark controls appearance only. Uncertain reconstruction stays `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
