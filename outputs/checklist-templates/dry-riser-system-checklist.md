## A. System Information

- Site / area
- Riser type: Dry Riser / Wet Riser / Combined
- Breeching inlet location
- Total dry/wet outlet quantity
- Pump system applicable: Yes / No
- Jockey pump applicable: Yes / No
- Duty pump applicable: Yes / No
- Standby pump applicable: Yes / No
- Last service date

## G. Final Restoration

- Confirm all landing valves are shut when not in use.
- Confirm bleeding valves are in correct normal position.
- Confirm breeching inlet caps are secured.
- Confirm cabinets are closed and secured.
- Inform Facilities Manager / client representative testing completed.

- Cabinet damaged
- Parts recommended
